# Best Creative Writing Courses Online in 2025 (Free and Paid)

Online creative writing education has never been better. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced writer looking to sharpen specific skills, there is a course designed for exactly where you are.

## What Makes a Good Creative Writing Course

Before the list: the criteria that separate good courses from generic content.

- **Instructor credentials:** Is this taught by someone who actually writes and publishes?
- **Feedback mechanism:** Do you receive feedback on your work, or just watch videos?
- **Specificity:** A course on "short story structure" is more useful than one on "becoming a writer"
- **Actionable exercises:** Does the course give you prompts and assignments, or just theory?

## Best Paid Courses

### MasterClass — Creative Writing

**Cost:** $120/year (all-access pass)

MasterClass is the most visually polished creative writing education available. The instructors are legends — Neil Gaiman, Margaret Atwood, Joyce Carol Oates, James Patterson, Shonda Rhimes. Neil Gaiman's course on storytelling is particularly outstanding.

What MasterClass does well: inspiration, high-level craft philosophy, learning how successful writers think. What it doesn't do: provide feedback on your work. You watch videos and complete exercises, but no one evaluates your writing.

**Verdict:** Excellent supplementary education. Not a substitute for a course with real feedback.

### Gotham Writers Workshop

**Cost:** $395-$595 per 10-week session

Gotham is the gold standard for serious creative writing education online. Each course runs 10 weeks, includes weekly assignments, and provides detailed feedback from a professional instructor plus peer feedback from classmates.

Courses are available for fiction, memoir, screenwriting, and children's books at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels.

**Verdict:** The closest thing to an MFA workshop available online. If you're serious and can afford it, Gotham is worth every dollar.

### Coursera — Creative Writing Specialization (Wesleyan University)

**Cost:** ~$49/month or audit free

Wesleyan's Creative Writing Specialization covers fiction, memoir, poetry, and the creative writing capstone. Taught by actual university faculty with peer-reviewed assignments.

**Verdict:** Great value, especially on audit. The certificate carries some weight for academic applications.

### Reedsy Learning

**Cost:** Free (email course format)

Reedsy has built an impressive library of free email courses covering everything from "How to Write a Novel" to "Writing Dialogue" to "How to Self-Publish." The instructors are publishing professionals — editors, agents, and authors.

**Verdict:** The best free creative writing education available anywhere. Start here if you're not ready to pay.

## Best Free Resources

### Brandon Sanderson's Creative Writing Lectures (YouTube)

Brandon Sanderson recorded his full university creative writing course and posted it free on YouTube. The lectures cover plot, characters, world-building, and the business of writing. One of the most comprehensive free educations available, particularly strong for genre fiction.

### NaNoWriMo (nanowrimo.org)

National Novel Writing Month provides free community support for writing a novel in 30 days. The forums, pep talks from published authors, and accountability structure have helped hundreds of thousands of writers finish first drafts.

## How to Choose

- **Most value for money:** Reedsy Learning (free) or Coursera audit (free)
- **Best instruction and feedback:** Gotham Writers Workshop
- **Inspiration from world-class authors:** MasterClass
- **Genre fiction (fantasy, sci-fi):** Brandon Sanderson's YouTube lectures
- **Finishing a novel:** NaNoWriMo community

## Can Online Courses Really Teach Creative Writing?

Partially. Online courses can teach craft — structure, dialogue, point of view, scene construction. What they cannot do is replace the hours of actual writing. The best creative writing education combines learning a craft concept with immediately applying it, seeking feedback, revising, and repeating.

Start writing. The courses will help. The writing will teach you more than any of them.
